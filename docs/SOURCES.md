- Paizo, **Pathfinder Second Edition Remaster** orientation: https://paizo.com/pathfinder/remaster/faq and free rules index via https://paizo.com/pathfinder/getstarted . The project does **not** reproduce Pathfinder rulebook text, official adventures, artwork, creatures, trade dress, or proprietary setting material. Review ORC licensing before importing any verbatim material: https://paizo.com/orclicense .

## Content boundary
All named places, adventures, lore, NPCs, encounter cards and house-rule prose here are new campaign material. Familiar game terms (d20, AC, HP, DC, advantage, action, bonus action, etc.) are used to explain game mechanics. This is **not** a complete copy of either system's core books; official system compatibility is not certified.

Before adding external text, art, data tables or a full stat-block dataset, identify that source's actual license and obligations, mark adaptations and attribution, and exclude reserved identities or material not authorized for reuse. Do not assume a free website makes content freely redistributable.
