# Places and geography

## The Hollow March
An old hill-and-fen district beyond the comfortable lowlands. Dry stone walls divide fields. Summer fog tastes of iron. The **Ashen Road** is a cobbled trading route that sometimes loses one mile near dusk. Bridges, bells and survey stakes disagree about where that mile belongs.

## Briarford — home base
A stone bridge over the Reedwater, twenty houses, a mill, a smith, a small market and a keeper's office. Everyone knows someone who works the road. Normal goods are available, but no magic emporium. Recent traffic fell after two carts arrived early and a third did not arrive at all.

**Places in town:** The Soot & Thistle inn (rumors, safe rest); Guild Survey Room (maps and paid job); South Bridge (witnesses); Warden Lantern Post (permits, warning); Reedwater landing (ferry and alternate route).

## The Vanishing Mile
Two miles outside Briarford, the road forks around flooded pasture, reconverging at a ruined tollhouse. On mist evenings a third path appears between them. The first adventure uses its **north gate**, **reed track**, **wayhouse**, **bell chamber** and **abandoned milestone**. Exact scene links are in [scenes.json](../adventures/01-vanishing-mile/scenes.json).

## Further regions (directional only; not yet playable modules)
- **Salt Road:** eastern market caravans that still pay the old tolls. An arc for levels 3–4 once adventures are authored.
- **The Crown of Rain:** remote upland towers and flooded archives. A future campaign arc, not an on-the-spot lore dump.

## Consistency rules
Road-shifting is localized near dusk and tied to active resonant bells; it does **not** grant time travel, arbitrary teleportation or a general-purpose spell system. The situation may become more complex through played discoveries, logged as new canon.
