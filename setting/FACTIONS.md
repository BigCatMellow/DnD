# Factions — usable drives, not fixed alignments

## Lantern Wardens
**Public role:** maintain waymarkers, protect trade and rescue stranded travelers.
**Real dilemma (GM):** old warding bells require a living memory freely or coercively surrendered. Some Wardens hide this cost.
**Goal:** keep the road stable through winter; **fear:** a complete collapse; **leverage:** maps, patrols, permits.
**Response:** helps travelers who preserve the bell; turns wary if ritual is exposed; may split into reform and hardline wings.

## Farbound Survey
**Public role:** independent cartographers and explorers.
**Goal:** map the true lost mile and publish the result; **fear:** maps being edited by authorities; **leverage:** records, tools, outsider allies.
**Response:** backs any evidence-based solution; some members risk lives recklessly to be first.

## Reedwater Guild
**Public role:** boatmen and fen communities bypassing the road.
**Goal:** preserve safe passage without magic tolls; **fear:** loss of livelihood and drowned homes; **leverage:** marsh routes and oral histories.
**Response:** welcomes allies who protect people; resists Warden expansion but will trade information.

## Faction state
Track attitude `hostile | wary | neutral | open | allied` per faction, plus explicit events in `state.json`. An attitude is an outcome of player action, never a hidden “reputation score” guessed in a later session.
