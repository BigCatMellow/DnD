# The Hollow March — setting router

Original low-fantasy frontier built around disappearing roads, lapsed promises and bells that hold memories.

- [PLACES](PLACES.md): region, settlement, routes and discoverable locations.
- [FACTIONS](FACTIONS.md): three groups, conflicting incentives and clocks.
- [PEOPLE](PEOPLE.md): GM-ready goals, voices, fears and leverage.
- [First adventure](../adventures/01-vanishing-mile/GM.md): hidden scenario-specific truth; **spoilers**.

**Player-known starting premise:** Travelers vanish along the Ashen Road when the fog comes down. The distances on maps never quite agree. Briarford offers modest pay to find a missing surveyor and bring back proof of the road's latest change.

Theme: the cost of holding on to a dream, and what a journey gives back beyond its destination. Use this as atmosphere, not a predetermined moral.
