# People — roleplay cards

## Ada Vell — Lantern Warden captain
**Voice:** precise, polite, weary; avoids speculation. **Wants:** keep traders alive this season. **Fears:** the entire road going dark. **Knows:** the tollhouse bell was recently reactivated, and its charge is failing. **Will bargain:** permit and patrol support in exchange for a quiet repair. **Will resist:** open destruction that threatens immediate passage. **Tell:** counts absentmindedly to nine when anxious.

## Orren Fen — guild archivist
**Voice:** matter-of-fact, dry humor. **Wants:** an honest map that matches witnesses. **Fears:** losing proof again. **Knows:** four versions of the same mile exist; one records a footpath through reeds. **Will bargain:** partial survey notes for a sketch or trustworthy witness account. **Tell:** writes dates in the margins of maps.

## Nessa Darr — Reedwater boatkeeper
**Voice:** warm but skeptical, talks around an offer once to test sincerity. **Wants:** safe river crossings and no more disappearances. **Fears:** toll collectors on the water. **Knows:** the drowned bell can be heard underwater before it is heard on land. **Will bargain:** ferry passage for help clearing a snag or a simple fair price. **Tell:** never whistles in mist.

## Lio Quill — young surveyor
**Voice:** quick, funny until questions turn personal. **Wants:** locate the missing senior surveyor and prove their maps correct. **Fears:** they caused the road to shift by moving a marker. **Knows:** they loosened the iron seal at the old tollhouse. **Will bargain:** map, measuring cord, and their company if trusted. **Tell:** sometimes forgets the name of a familiar place.

## Elowen Rusk — missing senior surveyor
**Voice:** lucid, determined, increasingly disoriented. **Wants:** be found without condemning someone else. **Fears:** surrendering the memory of why they began surveying. **Knows:** the ritual can be redirected through an unmarked milestone. **Availability:** the adventure determines whether they are found physically, as an echo, or too late for an easy rescue. **Tell:** keeps counting steps in groups of twelve.

## Pip Merrow — optional companion
**Voice:** observant, wry, never omniscient. **Wants:** see past the bridge and come home with a story. **Fears:** deep water, being useless. **Knows:** practical knots, local gossip, and no hidden GM solution. **Agency:** assists the hero, may suggest a concern, but player chooses the direction. **Stats:** [character data](../pregens/characters.json).
