# Choose a level-2 hero

Stats and starting gear are canonical in [characters.json](characters.json).

- **Mara Thorn — Guardian:** durable sword-and-shield fighter, straightforward decisions. HP 22, AC 17.
- **Iven Kest — Scout:** stealth, traps and opportunistic strikes. HP 17, AC 15.
- **Tamsin Vale — Trailwarden:** wilderness detective and ranged attacker. HP 20, AC 15.
- **Sera Flint — Resonance Scholar:** fragile mage specializing in clues and control. HP 14, AC 12.

All are level 2. You may rename and rewrite personality/backstory while preserving the mechanics. **Optional Pip Merrow** (light support NPC) can accompany any hero; the GM should not assume the player accepted the offer.

To begin: say `Play Mara with Pip`, `Play Iven alone`, etc. For custom creation, specify theme/class and build a versioned level-2 sheet before play begins.

Each hero has one healing potion. Check [spells and gear](../rules/SPELLS_GEAR.md) for precise effects. When leveling, consult [CHARACTERS](../rules/CHARACTERS.md).
