# Adventure 01: The Vanishing Mile
*Player-facing opening — no GM secrets.*

**Where:** Briarford, a bridge village on the edge of the Hollow March.

**The job:** A surveyor named Elowen Rusk left to measure a road that sometimes loses a mile. Elowen has not come home. The town offers **15 silver pieces** to anyone who returns with credible news. The road has a north gate and a slower path through the reeds.

**Mood:** Weathered inns, odd maps, strange bells, people with motives worth understanding. Investigation and conversation matter as much as fighting. Your choices can change the region.

**How to play:** Choose one of four [level-2 heroes](../../pregens/INDEX.md), with or without optional sidekick Pip. The GM describes the situation, rolls checks when needed, runs enemies and maintains the save. You may attempt anything plausible; listed choices are suggestions.

**Starting scene:** The Soot & Thistle's front room smells of wet wool and wood smoke. A town keeper has placed two contradictory maps on your table. Outside, the last daylight is turning the bridge stones amber. From the far side of the river, a bell rings nine times, though nobody has touched the village bell.

The keeper points at a name written twice in the map's margin: **Elowen Rusk**.

"Find out what happened," she says. "Bring back proof. And don't trust the miles."

You could question the keeper, inspect the maps, visit the survey office or leave toward the road. **Or do something else.**
