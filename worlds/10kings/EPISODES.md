# Episodic roadmap (provisional, not fixed Wurdren itinerary)

01 **The Bathhouse and the Cart**: prepared and playable now; Darkroot Gulch, local fuel dispute and a brief physical emergency.

02 **A Mule with Two Permits**: seed only; Highridge crossing and a practical journey complicated by two authorities. Must not duplicate the plot of the existing Sunday story The Road in Use.

03 **The Watch That Wasn't Paid**: seed only; a Northwind neighborhood night watch, a possible chase, debts, and everyday camaraderie.

04 **Someone Remembered**: seed only; build from actual relationships and consequences of previous sessions rather than inventing Wurdren's family or destiny.

The next episode is designed after play evidence and a new 10Kings canon check. Rotating regions, types of problem, physical challenges and supporting casts matters more than an arbitrary total number of quests. Wurdren's journey remains a garden, not a prescribed route.
