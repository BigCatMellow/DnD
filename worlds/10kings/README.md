## Interactive goal
Player makes all decisions for Wurdren, while GM controls world consequences, dice and everyone else. Humor comes from specific people's conflicting priorities; dangerous moments are short, material and clear. Each episode includes conversation, food or ordinary life, physical stakes when appropriate, a choice that changes someone's life, and a soft landing. Wurdren participates more often than in the canonical Sunday short-story collection because the game is explicitly from his viewpoint.
