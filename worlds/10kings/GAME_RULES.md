# Play rules — Wurdren / 10Kings, v0.1

**Source priority:** current 10Kings canon > this game-specific profile > shared basic d20 checks and mundane turn structure in [CORE](../../rules/CORE.md) and [COMBAT](../../rules/COMBAT.md). Do not import the Ashen Road spell list, undead or other fantasy creatures, magic items, faction lore or companions.

## Player-corrected identity
Wurdren is a **dwarf cleric**, confirmed by the player during episode 01. Those facts supersede the earlier generic 'seasoned traveler' class label in this campaign. His precise faith, religious order, home, age and family remain open. A D&D cleric ordinarily has spells, whereas canonical Two Sons explicitly disallows spellcasting: **do not quietly erase his class or invent divine spells**. Treat the incompatibility as a pending mechanics decision, and continue with ordinary physical and social actions meanwhile. Until then, retain the existing provisional HP/AC/attacks and mundane abilities, but do not claim they are a completed D&D cleric build. Dwarf ancestry is player-established in this game; do not invent darkvision, poison resistance or racial history without a deliberate later adaptation.

## Dice
An uncertain, consequential action uses d20 + ability/skill against DC 10 (routine under pressure), 13 (standard), 16 (hard) or 19 (exceptional). Advantage: roll twice, take higher; disadvantage: lower. A noncombat success by 5+ earns a concrete extra benefit. A failed essential investigation still produces a useful clue, usually at a cost. Never roll just because someone speaks, eats, walks or thinks. Announce the fictional stakes and show the roll/math. Obtain actual randomness from a reliable tool when possible, or ask the player to roll. Never invent dice results.

## Combat / danger
Use one action, movement, one reaction, and a bonus action only if a named ability allows it, per [COMBAT](../../rules/COMBAT.md). Initiative d20+Dex. Attack d20 + listed bonus vs AC; natural 20 doubles damage dice, natural 1 misses. HP is an abstraction for fatigue and being hurt, not a metaphysical substance. Nonlethal scuffles end at 0 HP with surrender, restraint, injury, broken gear or retreat. Clearly identified genuinely lethal danger uses ordinary death saves and real consequences; signal it BEFORE commitment, not afterward. No guaranteed safety by fudging rolls. Opponents are people and animals with lives, reasons, and morale; never automatic fights.

## Provisional veteran sheet
[Wurdren's numbers](character.json) are a practical solo-play interpretation only, not book canon. He is aging, not frail or supernatural, and can still get tired and be wrong. No spells, divine powers, alchemy heal potions or mythical equipment. Catch Breath and Steady Guard are mundane gameplay abstractions.

A safe hour permits a short rest; spend a remaining d10 hit die + Constitution modifier for HP recovery and recharge the two listed abilities. A safe eight-hour sleep is a long rest: restore HP and half of expended hit dice (at least one). Persistent wounds still need real care and time. Track food and money only when meaningful; keep material limits honest.

## Sunday storytelling and agency
Stay with a contained community, its working habits, real tradeoffs and an ordinary meal. Physical danger may occasionally spike (cart, chase, brawl, difficult rescue), with breathing room after. Dialogue is specific, conversational and not a chain of clever punchlines. Narrator has character but leaves jokes to people. Withhold explanations of the larger network while keeping spatial mechanics clear. Wurdren's internal thoughts, chosen words, moral conclusions and history belong to the player.

The wider conflict is only a small material pressure in the local episode; no character uncovers the Council or the Villain by a convenient skill check. Attribution remains UNKNOWN unless separately established by canon. Every novel-world addition here is explicitly game-only until promoted by the human in a separate approved task.

Commands: status, inventory, journal, recap, pause, rewind, fade out, rules. Never force a choice from a menu; any plausible action is allowed. Save after meaningful decisions, and say when GitHub was not synchronized.
