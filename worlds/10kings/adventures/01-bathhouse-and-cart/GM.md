# GM-only — Episode 01: The Bathhouse and the Cart

**Status:** Provisional in-game story, NOT 10Kings canon. Based loosely on the parked Sunday-Morning idea [The Bathhouse Compact](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/10Kings/blob/main/wiki/Sunday-Morning/Stories/Story-Seeds.md#the-bathhouse-compact). Setting owner for Darkroot: [Border Towns](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/10Kings/blob/main/wiki/Places/Border-Towns.md). Source authority: [worlds/10kings/README](../../README.md). Local hazards: [hazards.json](hazards.json). Scene graph: [scenes.json](scenes.json).

## What is happening
Darkroot Gulch's only bathhouse serves charcoal burners and woodland wardens. Both groups have histories and legitimate claims to warmth. A rise in paid kiln contracts has redirected charcoal loads into Ironcrest metalwork; the bathhouse's traditional reserve was bumped in the schedule. **We do not know** who ultimately arranged the contracts. The mule cart's brake was secured carelessly by an exhausted driver, Keld Fenner. It is not sabotage and has nothing supernatural behind it.

## Provisional cast
- **Hessa Rill**, bathkeeper. Wants the place open and people fed; uses distinctions about water temperature mostly to avoid refunds. Has a real fear of splitting neighbors into incompatible bath schedules. Keeps a pot of broth and bread available. Wry when busy, quieter with those who need kindness.
- **Keld Fenner**, charcoal drayer. Wants to keep his mule, cart and wages; knows the delivery order was changed. Is careless today because a paid night run extended his shift, not because he is malicious. He can own the error if treated fairly. Useful at repairs.
- **Vena Sedge**, boundary forest warden. Wants woodland workers to retain access to a common facility, not be quietly outbid. Knows how to insulate the washhouse and where to find spare flue metal. She distrusts the buyers, not every charcoal worker.

## Opening spatial adjudication
Player starts in bathhouse yard. Cart and mule are forty feet upslope on 15-foot-wide cobbles; it has just started rolling, not yet arrived. Bathhouse doorway 10 feet behind Wurdren is clear. A drystone drainage strip along lane allows access to cart's flank. Two alert adult workers near outer gate will move if warned. The cart threatens the low rail and a pot, not a crowd trapped with no escape.

Before touching dice, ask how Wurdren acts. Immediate clear warning moves workers to safety without a roll. An interception, brake rescue, harness grab, cart diversion, or improvised wedging generally uses DC13 Athletics or other clearly justified skill, with advantage for good preparation or qualified help. Full success: no personal harm and rail spared. Strong success: save load/mule from distress as well. Narrow failure: workers clear but some charcoal spills, or property damage; if Wurdren chose a physically risky position, 1d4 HP damage from a bruise. Failure by 5+: rail bends and more fuel spills, with Wurdren able to retreat. Never inflict random death, force an action or invent a successful roll. If player does nothing, adults react to noise and cart damages rail; consequences still playable.

After resolution move to 'aftermath'; first action should update save and journal. Hessa offers bread or broth. Give a genuine short quiet scene before escalating.

## Nonlinear exploration
- **aftermath**: Hessa describes old rota. Keld explains changed order and his fatigue. Vena gives a different perspective. No roll needed to hear their concerns. A valid social approach can earn trust; hostile posture may lose cooperation but not basic information.
- **washroom**: the old flue leaks heat. Hessa feeds everyone a simple meal if possible. The washhouse might open for a limited time if fuel is saved, a fair allocation made or supplies arrive. No skill gate to know it leaks.
- **kiln_lane**: an apparently legitimate higher-paying order took the expected charcoal. The carter's supervisor wants to honor paid work, not personally hurt bathers. A document can be examined; DC13 Investigation gets a useful detail about dates; even failure shows the actual destination and a chance to negotiate. Optional nonlethal shoving or chase only if the player acts to provoke or prevent it; use [hazards](hazards.json).
- **sluice**: Vena can help repair a short flue gap or add reused insulation. DC13 Athletics/Investigation (or plausible tool check); failure costs materials/time but still makes partial progress. A safe repair makes a limited opening possible even without obtaining more charcoal.

## Outcomes, not railroad endings
- **ending_open**: People agree to a reduced schedule, alternate supply, a repair or some combination. Participants must genuinely agree; a Persuasion check cannot erase competing interests. Vena/Hessa/Keld remember specific favors and losses. A local compromise may send a rumor to another town, but no known Villain/Council proof.
- **ending_closed**: The facility temporarily closes; neighbors find alternate washing and heating, and the disagreement survives. Wurdren's relationships may still improve. The story is not a failure just because it isn't tidy.
- **Emergent solution**: If player finds plausible creative third path, honor it and record a free-form outcome in state/journal. The graph has two prebuilt terminals; never restrict action to just those.

## Mood and world-tie constraints
Warm, conversational, slightly irreverent. People are worried about fuel, wages, fairness and a meal, not global cataclysm. Distinct character humor, not a stream of punchlines. One energetic moment in yard; later action only if earned. Include a resting meal and a breath of normal life. Don't diagnose Wurdren's emotions, retcon his open biography, invent magic, or reveal the larger political network. The broader supply pattern matches [Current Events](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/10Kings/blob/main/wiki/Story/Current-Events.md#unusual-metal-movements), but attribution remains UNKNOWN and game-only.

## GM checkpoints
Read state before each turn. Track HP, actual rolls, clues, clocks and relationship shifts. Give 2–3 natural examples for next action, not a compulsory menu. After a consequential turn, update state and journal on campaign branch; if unable, present an unsynced save and say so. Story ends on the player's decision and the social consequences, not the narrator's moral lecture.
