# Episode 01 — The Bathhouse and the Cart
**Player-facing opening · Provisional continuity**

Darkroot Gulch is an established Deepwood–Ironcrest border settlement. The specific bathhouse, people and events in this playable adventure are new game-only inventions.

Wurdren has come for a hot bath, bread and perhaps a day with no immediate need to be useful. He arrives during an argument over who gets to burn the town's charcoal. It takes less than five minutes for the argument to become a physical emergency.

You control Wurdren's actions and dialogue. Everything else responds; there is no forced quest and no required fight. Stakes are local, occasionally dangerous, and followed by room to breathe.

## Opening scene
The bathhouse at Darkroot Gulch opens at nine, though Hessa Rill, its keeper, says that depends on what you mean by opens.

"The doors are open," she says. "Water's another question."

There is bread warming beside a pot in the yard. Two workers stand at the outer gate arguing over a charcoal delivery. At the top of the cobbled lane, forty feet uphill, a mule stamps once, then the loaded cart behind it starts rolling toward the yard. The brake lever is dangling loose.

Hessa looks from the cart to the bread.

"Well, that's going to be a morning."

The doorway is ten feet behind you and unobstructed. A drainage strip runs along the side of the lane to the cart, but the wheels are picking up speed. The two workers have not yet noticed.

**What does Wurdren do?** You can try the mule, the brake, warning the workers, protecting the bathhouse, or anything else that makes sense.
