# Starter spells and gear (house implementation)

These are concise encounter-ready effects used by the **pregens**, not a full SRD spell compendium. Spell attacks use listed spell attack bonus; saves use listed spell DC. **Concentration:** at most one concentration effect; when taking damage, Constitution save DC 10 or half damage (higher), ending effect on failure. Concentration ends when unconscious. A spell's duration is as listed. The GM never invents extra castings or spell slots.

## Cantrips, unlimited
- **Spark:** action, ranged spell attack within 60 ft; `1d10` fire on hit.
- **Whisperlight:** action, create or dismiss a palm-sized light illuminating 20 ft around you for 1 hour; can be covered.
- **Mage Hand:** action, manipulate an unattended object up to 10 lb within 30 ft; no attacks, locks or traps by itself.
- **Minor Illusion:** action, create a stationary sound or image within 30 ft for 1 minute; Investigation vs spell DC to recognize it on inspection.
- **Guiding Word:** action, a willing ally within 30 ft gains `1d4` on its next ability check in 1 minute; not usable on your own check, no stacking.

## Level-1 spells (spend one slot)
- **Arcane Ward:** reaction when hit by an attack or targeted by magic dart; +5 AC including triggering attack until start of your next turn.
- **Sleep Dust:** action, one creature within 60 ft makes Wisdom save or suffers disadvantage on its next attack roll before end of next turn; not unconscious. This is an original weakened solo-friendly variant.
- **Mending Pulse:** action, touch a creature for `1d8+3` HP healing (cannot exceed maximum).
- **Detect Resonance:** action, concentration up to 10 minutes; sense active supernatural bell-magic within 30 ft, but not through thick stone; no identification of hidden creature types.
- **Entangling Thread:** action, creature within 60 ft makes Strength save or is Restrained until end of its next turn. Subsequent action to repeat save and break free; concentration up to 1 minute.
