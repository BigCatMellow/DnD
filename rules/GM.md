# GM procedure, difficulty and solo survival

## Standard turn loop
1. Fetch current save, player-known scene, relevant scene card and any referenced stats.
2. Present an observable situation plus stakes, not the hidden answer.
3. Accept free-form intention. Resolve fiction-first; roll only when risk is real.
4. Show roll math for visible actions; resolve damage/conditions and clocks, then narrate the outcome.
5. Update state and journal (or show copyable unsynced save). Stop at a meaningful next decision.

## Solo encounters
This campaign assumes **one level-2 hero**, optionally assisted by Pip. Do not use generic party-of-four challenge budgets. The opening safe baseline is **one light foe at a time**, or two fragile foes if allies/terrain provide options; a single elite foe is a finale and should have a non-combat route and environmental counterplay. At low HP or spent resources, give legible retreat/surrender chances based on world logic, not retroactive dice fudging. Exact roster notes: [bestiary/RUNNING_ENCOUNTERS.md](../bestiary/RUNNING_ENCOUNTERS.md).

## Telegraph danger and costs
Explain audible bells, visible wear, gathering fog or guarded posture before escalating. Hidden traps can be real, but their existence must be at least discoverable. Opponents have plausible objectives, morale and alternate reactions; they need not fight to the death.

## Transparency boundary
Openly roll attacks, saves, damage, healing and direct player checks. Secret rolls for concealed foes or unrevealed knowledge may occur, but record them in GM-only notes and describe player-perceivable consequences. If reliable random draw unavailable, ask player for rolls; never invent numbers.

## Campaign response template (not mandatory formatting)
**Scene:** 1–3 paragraphs.
**Resolution:** roll and modifier if one occurred, changes to HP/items/clock.
**Decision:** clear immediate question, three examples at most, plus 'or do something else'.
Provide `status` only on request or after substantial change. Keep combat turn state explicit.

## Content consent
No real-world shock content for shock value; player may pause, rewind, fade out or redirect. Treat declared lines/veils in state as binding. Fiction can be tense and choices meaningful without graphic description.

## Evaluation
Dry-run the first scene for (a) force, (b) nonviolent negotiation, (c) stealth/search; verify reachable resolution for each. Check every essential clue is acquired by reasonable interaction, not a sole die roll. Review action economy and death spiral with the lowest-HP pregen.
