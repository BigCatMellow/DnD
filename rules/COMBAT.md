# Combat (v0.1)

## Turn order and time
At the start, each combatant rolls initiative **d20 + Dexterity modifier**; ties favor higher Dexterity, then player, then GM. One round is approximately six seconds.

Each turn: **movement** up to Speed, **one action**, **one bonus action if an ability provides one**, **one free brief interaction**, and speech of a reasonable length. A creature has **one reaction** between the start of its turn and the start of its next turn. Movement can split around actions. The Pathfinder three-action system is **not** used.

Common actions: Attack, Cast a spell (as its text permits), Dash (move Speed again), Disengage (do not provoke from moving away this turn), Dodge (attacks against you have disadvantage if you can perceive attackers; Dexterity saves advantage), Help (ally gets advantage on next relevant attack/check if plausible), Hide (Dexterity Stealth vs passive Perception or GM DC), Use an object, Ready (declare trigger and action; uses reaction when triggered).

**Opportunity attack:** when a visible adjacent target leaves your reach voluntarily without Disengaging, you may spend reaction on one melee attack. Forced movement does not trigger. Default reach 5 feet. **Ranged attacks in melee:** disadvantage if a hostile creature is within 5 feet that can see you and is not incapacitated.

**Attack:** d20 + attack bonus vs target Armor Class (AC). Natural 20 hits and doubles the weapon/spell damage dice, then adds modifier once; natural 1 misses. A natural 20 never doubles static bonuses. Damage reduces HP. At 0 HP see below. **Cover:** half cover +2 AC and Dexterity saves; three-quarters cover +5; total cover cannot be targeted directly.

## Condition summaries
- **Prone:** crawling uses doubled movement; standing costs half Speed. Melee attacks within 5 ft have advantage; other attacks against prone targets have disadvantage; prone attacks have disadvantage.
- **Grappled:** Speed 0. Escape as action, Strength Athletics or Dexterity Acrobatics against grappler's listed escape DC; ends if grappler incapacitated or target leaves reach.
- **Restrained:** Speed 0; attacks against you advantage, your attacks disadvantage, Dexterity saves disadvantage.
- **Frightened:** disadvantage on checks and attacks while fear source visible; cannot willingly move closer to source.
- **Poisoned:** disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
- **Disoriented** (original): disadvantage on your next **ability check** before the end of your next turn, then ends. No action loss.
- **Unconscious:** incapacitated, can't move/speak, unaware; drops held items, falls prone; attackers within 5 ft that hit score a critical hit.
Effects do not stack with themselves; an entry supplies its duration and recovery.

## 0 HP, death and healing
A player at 0 HP falls unconscious and starts death saves on their turns: raw d20 DC 10, three successes stabilize (at 0 HP), three failures die; natural 1 counts as two failures, natural 20 restores 1 HP. Damage taken at 0 HP adds one failed save (two on a critical hit); damage equal to or exceeding maximum HP in one blow kills outright by default. Healing above 0 ends unconscious and resets death-save counts. NPCs and monsters normally fall at 0 unless GM flags a named NPC as having death saves before combat.

**Not fudging:** dice stand. Enemies may surrender, flee, or shift goals according to established motivations, not to retroactively rescue a hero.

## Position and scale
Use **theatre of the mind** by default. Track zones: engaged (within 5 ft), near (within 30 ft), far (31–60 ft), distant (more than 60 ft). State if one movement reaches the target; for precise disputes use feet and Speed from the creature sheet. A foe within five feet is engaged. Difficult terrain costs two feet movement per foot. Lighting/visibility are adjudicated with [EXPLORATION](EXPLORATION.md).

## End of encounter
Award story progress by milestones, not XP arithmetic. Check conditions and consumed resources before writing save state. Encounter rosters and scaling live in [bestiary/RUNNING_ENCOUNTERS.md](../bestiary/RUNNING_ENCOUNTERS.md).
