# Characters, rest and progression

## Quick start
Select one [level-2 pregen](../pregens/INDEX.md). Character sheet numbers in `pregens/characters.json` are canonical for that hero; do not recalculate or silently customize them. Rename, description, pronouns and non-mechanical history can be freely chosen. Character creation from scratch is optional later.

## Baseline
A character has Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma; HP, AC, Speed, proficient skills and saves, attacks, class abilities, inventory, money and limited-use resources. Proficiency bonus is **+2** at levels 1–4. A character may use weapons or skills even when not proficient unless an ability specifically requires proficiency.

**Capacity:** avoid encumbrance counting for ordinary kits. A character can reasonably carry their starting gear plus a few small finds; conspicuously oversized burdens prompt a fictional choice.

## Rests
- **Short rest:** one uninterrupted hour in relative safety. Spend up to remaining hit dice (a level-2 character has two), rolling each character's hit-die size plus Constitution modifier, healing at least 1 per die; spent dice do not return until long rest. Abilities marked short-rest reset. The GM should make pursuit/noise consequences clear.
- **Long rest:** eight hours including sleep in a safe place; return to full HP, spell slots, long-rest features, and up to half total hit dice (minimum one). Only one long rest grants these benefits per 24 hours.
- **Healing potion:** action to drink/administer, restores `2d4+2` HP; no effect on death. Stabilized at 0 HP requires healing to regain consciousness.

## Leveling
Level by **milestone**, normally after adventure resolution, not per kill. The v0.1 pregens are mechanically defined only for level 2. Before advancing to level 3, publish the selected hero's level-3 update (HP, proficiency, features, spells) and validate it. Do not invent a level-up during a scene. Until then, milestone can unlock narrative rewards.

## Companion
Optional sidekick **Pip Merrow** may accompany the hero to make solo play less lethal. Pip is run by the GM, but the player can direct broad tactics. Pip is never the protagonist, cannot solve a core mystery unaided, and will not die offscreen as a random consequence. Exact statistics and behavior: [pregens/characters.json](../pregens/characters.json).
