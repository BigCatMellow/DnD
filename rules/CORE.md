# Core checks (v0.1)

**Roll:** d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus (only if proficient) + explicit situational modifier. Meet or exceed the Difficulty Class (DC). Most level-2 player checks use proficiency **+2**. Ability modifier = floor((score - 10) / 2). Ability scores: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma.

| DC | Difficulty | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | Routine under pressure | Scale a wet fence |
| 13 | Standard | Read a guarded expression |
| 16 | Challenging | Disable a clever alarm |
| 19 | Expert | Decode a fading ancient map |

No roll for tasks without meaningful risk or where failure cannot affect the fiction. When stakes are present, the GM says the **type of risk** before rolling; a secret trap DC may be hidden, but not the possibility that a trap exists when perceptible.

**Advantage/disadvantage:** roll 2d20, keep higher/lower. They cancel; multiples do not stack. Preparation can grant advantage OR reduce DC by 2, not both for the same setup. Untrained characters still add their ability modifier; proficiency comes from sheet.

**Skill tests:** success at DC; strong success at DC+5 adds a concrete benefit. Failure by 1–4 incurs a cost or limited progress when plausible; failure by 5+ causes a stronger setback. This graduated approach applies to **noncombat ability checks only**; attacks and saves obey their own rules. A natural 20/1 is not automatically success/failure on an ability check.

**Opposition:** opposed checks are only used if two active parties are actually competing; higher total wins and ties favor the current defender. Prefer a fixed DC when that is simpler.

**Common proficiency categories:** Athletics (Str); Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth (Dex); Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion (Int); Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival (Wis); Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion (Cha). Improvisations use an ability and optionally one justified skill; no double proficiency.

**Saving throw:** d20 + named ability modifier + proficiency if the character is proficient in that saving throw. Meet stated DC; otherwise apply specified consequence. Graduated success is **not** applied to saves. Cover, concealment and environmental positioning are described in [COMBAT](COMBAT.md).

**Group/companion checks:** one designated lead rolls; qualified help may grant advantage if the companion can plausibly contribute. No free rerolls after a revealed result.

**Rounding:** round down unless specified otherwise. Dice notation `2d6+3` means roll two six-sided dice and add three.
